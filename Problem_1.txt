Run with Hadoop:
time hadoop jar wordcount2.jar WordCount2 /largerDataset/Wikipedia-EN-20120601_ARTICLES /Problem1/1a_ExtSort -skippatterns /Problem1/stopwords.txt

External Sorting:
hdfs dfs -cat /Problem1/1a_ExtSort/part-r-* | sort -k2 -nr | head -50 > top50.txt
